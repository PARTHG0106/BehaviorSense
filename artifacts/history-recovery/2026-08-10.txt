**1. The 4-stream ensemble — already trained, never evaluated.** You have four checkpoints. Every number you've seen is one stream in isolation. Notebook 04 already does the logit averaging and the prob-vs-logit ablation. The streams are genuinely decorrelated (joint/bone differ by 0.06, motion pair by another 0.05), which is the precondition for averaging to help. Published ST-GCN++ gains over the best single stream are +2 to +4 points, so expect roughly **0.21–0.23**. Cost: one short session, no retraining.

**2. Temporal smoothing — implemented, and this is the big one.** `classify_stream` runs Viterbi over a transition prior. Every number so far is a *2-second window in isolation*, but activities persist for minutes. A `using_phone` window misread as `reading` gets corrected by its neighbours. This is where per-window accuracy and per-segment accuracy diverge sharply, and **segments are what Agent 3 consumes** — window accuracy isn't the metric that matters downstream.

**3. Object context — implemented, not wired to RT-DETR.** `fuse_objects` applies log-odds priors and you already staged `rtdetr-l.onnx`. This targets precisely the classes that are failing.
