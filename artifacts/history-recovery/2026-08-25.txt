## What landed

**[multicorpus.py](src/behaviorsense/data/multicorpus.py)** — the piece that makes training on 365k Toyota windows *alongside* Charades safe rather than actively harmful.

The problem it solves: Toyota covers 15 of 22 coarse classes and structurally cannot cover the rest. It annotates `Sit_down` as a transition and never labels the sustained posture; a third of its frames are unannotated gaps where sitting is most likely. Under plain cross-entropy, every one of those 365,492 windows becomes a statement that the resident is **not** sitting — training the model to suppress exactly the classes Charades is there to teach.

`masked_cross_entropy` masks unsupervisable classes to `-inf` **before** the softmax. That distinction is the whole thing, and it's the tempting wrong implementation to get backwards: zeroing a masked class's loss *afterwards* leaves it in the normaliser, so predicting it is still penalised and the model still learns "Toyota windows aren't sitting."

Measured, in M1:

```
gradient on `sitting`, Toyota row:   0.000000   ← exactly zero, all 9 unsupervised classes
gradient on `sitting`, Charades row: -0.472222   ← still supervised
```

Also in place:

- **`CorpusSpec` per shard tag.** An unknown tag **raises** rather than defaulting to "supervise everything" — that default would silently reintroduce the bug for whichever corpus was added last. Toyota's supervised sets are *derived* from the maps, so adding a class can't leave the mask behind.
- **Union coverage asserted** (M4): all 22 classes reachable, and `falling` comes only from the fall corpora — never from Toyota, which contains no falls.
- **Charades trusted narrowly** (M5): postures kept because nothing else covers them, falls excluded, and the v1.1 appendages (`using_device`, `object_interaction`) excluded because those shards predate the classes.
- **Two separate fine heads** (M6), `tsm31` and `tsu51`. `Readbook`=23 and `Read`=19 are the same activity at different ids; `Cook.Cleanup` has no exact untrimmed counterpart. Merging them needs another hand-written label map — the exact thing that produced macro-F1 0.128 — so they stay separate as *auxiliary* supervision.
- **`pooled_coarse_logits` uses log-sum-exp** (M7): 11 equal `Cook.*` members pool to `log(11) = 2.398`. A mean would give 0.0 and systematically under-predict every class that happens to be split into many members.
- **Unsatisfiable targets raise at the call** (M2), instead of `+inf` loss and NaN weights minutes later with nothing in the log pointing at the label.
